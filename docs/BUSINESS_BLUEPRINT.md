# Lekturnik — Business Blueprint

## 1. Market Overview

### Total Addressable Market

Poland's education system delivers a structurally recurring customer base. According to GUS data for 2024/25:

| Segment | Students | Exam |
|---------|----------|------|
| Klasy 7-8 (szkola podstawowa) | ~550,000-700,000 | Egzamin osmoklasisty (mandatory Polish) |
| Licea ogolnoksztalcace | ~760,000 | Matura z polskiego (mandatory) |
| Technika | ~688,000 | Matura z polskiego (mandatory) |
| **Total TAM** | **~2.0-2.15 million** | |

Annual exam cohorts:
- ~250,000-255,000 matura students (255,517 in 2025)
- ~190,000-350,000 egzamin osmoklasisty students
- 2025 matura Polish: average 59% at poziom podstawowy, 6% failing

### Market Dynamics

- Poland's online education market: US$385.8M in 2025, projected US$442M by 2029
- **48% of Polish teens use AI for learning** (NASK "Nastolatki" 2024 survey, 3,655 students)
- **28% specifically summarize lektury with AI** (Empik parent survey, 2025)
- **70% of Polish teens use AI tools** generally
- Wikipedia usage for learning collapsed from 76% (2018) to 23% (2024), replaced by AI
- Strong "free content" culture — price sensitivity is acute
- Polish purchasing power runs 40-60% below Western European levels
- **38% of parents** report AI tools effectively motivate their children

## 2. Competitive Landscape

### Lekturowo — The Incumbent

- Solo dev (Pawel Sobaszek, SOMNISO STUDIO, near Bilgoraj)
- **500K+ Android installs**, 4.7-star rating, 1,170 reviews
- Static content: summaries, characters, event plans, reading quizzes, epoch overviews, motif analyses
- Premium: 9.99 PLN/month (audio, mind maps, highlighting, no ads). iOS: $1.99 one-time
- Estimated $200K-500K+ lifetime earnings
- **Zero AI features** in 6 years of development
- Critical gaps: no AI quizzes, no personalized study paths, no SRS, no essay prep, no matura analytics, no cross-lektura motif search

### The Gap

**No one combines verified, curriculum-aligned literary analysis with AI-powered interactive assessment.** ChatGPT hallucinates on Polish literature (fabricated plots and characters documented in multiple media reports). This trust gap is our moat.

### WolneLektury.pl as Content Accelerator

- 7,000+ ebooks and 1,200+ audiobooks, all public domain or CC BY-SA
- Includes most mandatory lektury with annotations and motif tagging
- CC BY-SA license allows commercial reuse under share-alike terms
- Strategic opportunity: combine their free texts with our AI-powered study tools

### Copyright Map

**Public domain:** Mickiewicz, Prus, Sienkiewicz, Fredro, Zeromski, Wyspianski, Reymont, Borowski, Tuwim

**Copyrighted (expiry year):**
- Camus *Dzuma* — 2031
- Gombrowicz *Ferdydurke* — 2040
- Herbert — 2069
- Szymborska — 2083
- Milosz — 2075
- Mrozek *Tango* — 2084
- Tokarczuk — living author

**Key nuance:** Even when original works are public domain, modern Polish **translations** of foreign authors (Shakespeare, Moliere, Dostoevsky, Orwell) may be copyrighted based on translator death dates.

**Prawo cytatu (Art. 29):** Short excerpts permissible with attribution, even in commercial contexts. Full poem reproduction legally ambiguous — requires copyright attorney opinion (~1,000-2,000 PLN).

## 4. Legal Compliance

### RODO/GDPR for Minors

- Poland's digital consent age: **16 years** (default, not lowered)
- Users 14-15: require parental consent when consent is legal basis
- Strategy: allow core features without accounts (local storage), require accounts for enhanced features, parental consent flow for under-16
- Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) or contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)) may avoid Art. 8 requirement
- UODO takes restrictive view on minors — professional legal opinion recommended (~500-1,500 PLN)

### CKE Matura Papers

- State institution, statutory mandate, published openly on cke.gov.pl
- Art. 4(2) Copyright Act: "official documents" excluded from copyright protection
- Multiple commercial sites already republish without enforcement
- **Caveat:** Literary texts embedded in exam papers retain own copyright
- Always attribute to CKE

### EU AI Act

- Education AI listed in Annex III as potentially high-risk
- Lekturnik likely falls outside high-risk (doesn't determine access or formally evaluate)
- **August 2026:** transparency requirements (Art. 50) mandate labeling AI-generated content
- Implement from launch: "Ta odpowiedz zostala wygenerowana przez AI"
